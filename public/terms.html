What this service does

QR Studio creates QR codes and optionally hosts small files temporarily. QR creation is offered without login or a QR-generation fee. There is no guarantee that an external destination remains accessible. File hosting is limited to 5 MB per file, 30 days of accessibility and capacity/rate safeguards, and may be temporarily unavailable.
