What this service does

QR Studio creates QR codes and optionally hosts small files temporarily. QR creation is offered without login or a QR-generation fee. There is no guarantee that an external destination remains accessible. File hosting is limited to 5 MB per file, 30 days of accessibility and capacity/rate safeguards, and may be temporarily unavailable.

Treat an uploaded file URL as public. Do not use the service for spam, phishing, evasion, or distribution of malware.

Service integrity

We may restrict uploads or remove files early to prevent abuse or comply with applicable legal requirements. Files expire automatically; do not rely on this service as a backup or long-term archive. Scanning and downloading QR-linked content is at the recipient's discretion. Test QR exports before distributing them.
