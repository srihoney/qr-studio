Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

Local QR generation

QR patterns for URLs, text, Wi-Fi and contact details are created in your browser. We do not store those QR payloads in a QR Studio account. Interface theme preference may be kept in your browser's local storage.

Optional temporary file uploads

If you use the file-upload feature, the file and its technical metadata are stored in Cloudflare R2 and D1. Anyone with the unique sharing link can access the file; an online-view mode can hide the download button but cannot prevent copying or screenshots. Files stop being served 30 days after upload and are scheduled for physical deletion; deletion jobs and storage lifecycle processing can take additional time. We do not promise files are confidential or safe for sensitive information. Basic, salted one-way hashes of network addresses are temporarily used to limit spam and abusive uploads.

Contact form

If you submit a message, your name, email address and text are sent to our mail provider and delivered to the site operator. QR Studio does not offer a public directory of those messages. The email provider and recipient's mailbox may retain them under their respective policies.

Security and infrastructure

Cloudflare serves the site, protects submission forms using Turnstile and may process technical request information under its own policies. The site may use privacy-focused Cloudflare Web Analytics if the owner enables it; access to analytics stays inside the Cloudflare account. The QR Studio service can include provider-required security cookies or similar security technologies.

Retention and requests

File links expire after 30 days; anti-abuse records are periodically removed. For early deletion, keep the private deletion receipt provided at upload. You can use it to remove your file without an account. For other privacy questions or reports of harmful material, use the contact form and include the file's URL if relevant. We respond when reasonably possible.
