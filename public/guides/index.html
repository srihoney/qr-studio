Before you share any QR code

  • Verify the destination or encoded data.
  • Test the exported or printed QR code with at least one real phone.
  • Use a reliable link if you expect a poster, menu or handout to remain usable for months.
  • Keep sufficient contrast, size and clear space around the QR pattern.
