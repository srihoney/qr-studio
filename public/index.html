GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

The essentials about sharing and access, in one place.

Does a QR code expire?

The static code does not expire on its own. The destination may change or become unavailable.

How long can I share an uploaded file?

QR Studio-hosted uploads are available for 30 days. After that, file access stops and the stored copy is deleted.

Who can access the file?

Anyone with its QR code or link. View Online hides the obvious download action but cannot prevent saving or screenshots. Uploaded files are not malware-scanned; avoid sensitive documents.

Can I remove my file sooner?

Uploaders can remove a file before expiry using their private deletion receipt in the deployed service.
