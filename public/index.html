USEFUL TO KNOW

Honest answers about expiration.

Do QR codes expire?

No. QR Studio generates static codes with no subscription or built-in expiration. A link can still become invalid when its target disappears, and QR Studio-hosted uploads stop working after 30 days.

What about large files?

Upload supported files of up to 5 MB for 30-day sharing, or paste a link from your own cloud storage for larger files.

Can someone edit a QR later?

Static QR data never changes. You can replace the file behind a stable link if your cloud provider preserves that URL; changing the URL requires a new QR.

Is free really free?

Yes. No login or QR-generation fees. Hosted uploads have a 5 MB file limit and operational quotas to keep the service sustainable.
