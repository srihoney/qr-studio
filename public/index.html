How to make a shareable Google Drive link
  1. Upload your file to Google Drive.
  2. Select the file, then click Share.
  3. Choose General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer (if allowed by your account).
  4. Copy that link and paste it above.
  5. Test the link in an incognito/private browser window before printing the QR.

USEFUL TO KNOW

Honest answers about expiration.

01

Do QR codes expire?

No. QR Studio generates static codes with no subscription or built-in expiration. A link can still become invalid if its target disappears.

02

What about large files?

Even a small PDF cannot realistically fit inside a QR. Upload it to a cloud service, allow viewing through a shared link, and paste the link here. We don't host files.

03

Can someone edit a QR later?

Static QR data never changes. You can replace the file behind a stable link if your cloud provider preserves that URL; changing the URL requires a new QR.

04

Is free really free?

Yes. No login, QR limits, watermarks or payment features. QR creation works in your browser. A cloud provider may have its own storage limits.
